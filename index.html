About me

I am an Information security graduate with an honors degree and a strong academic foundation built through intensive study and hands-on practice. During my studies, I developed a broad understanding of cybersecurity, networking, operating systems, and security technologies, while continuously expanding my knowledge through independent learning and practical labs.

My hands-on experience includes Active Directory and Group Policy, Windows and Linux environments, network analysis with Wireshark, security monitoring with Splunk, and vulnerability assessment using Nmap and Nessus. I have also worked with a range of cybersecurity tools and technologies, including Kali Linux, Metasploit, Burp Suite, and OWASP Juice Shop, while continuously exploring new tools and security techniques.

I am highly motivated to continue learning, develop my practical cybersecurity skills, and gain real-world experience. I enjoy investigating technical problems, understanding how systems work, and continuously improving my knowledge to build a strong career in Information Security.
